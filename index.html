The Forest Fire Ontology and its multimodal knowledge graph

A guide to the Forest Fire Ontology: how it was built from the questions people ask about wildfire images, sounds and weather, how its knowledge graph is constructed and validated, and how to query it, either with SPARQL or by asking FFOnto-Bot in plain English.
